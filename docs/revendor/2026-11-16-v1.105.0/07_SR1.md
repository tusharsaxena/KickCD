## Title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

KickCD draws **no title-bar controls of its own**, so the step was skipped and no test was added.

- The perf panel, the sim step panel, the debug console, the copy window and the skin picker are the
  library's windows; their marks, tooltips and colors come with the re-copy. `core/PerfSetup.lua`
  passes no `decorate` hook, and `modules/Sim.lua` only hides and re-shows the sim panel's library close
  mark while a session runs.
- The icon grids and cast bars are HUD frames with the library's `DragHandle` strip and its `?` mark.
  The strip and mark show the host's tooltip ("Drag to move. Right-click for settings."). Their colors
  are the library's.
- Noted, not changed: both strips pass `tooltipPlace = NS.Util.PlaceTooltipBeside`
  (`core/Util.lua`), which places the strip's tooltip beside the strip, to the right (or left), top
  aligned. The library honors a host hook that answers `true`, so these tooltips do not follow
  `SR-L1`'s "above, flip below". The owner's complaint (a tooltip below the control, under the
  pointer) does not apply to a side placement. Moving the strips onto `PlaceAttached` is part of
  LibKa0s#92's adoption ("its own tooltips through the placers"), not this re-vendor.
