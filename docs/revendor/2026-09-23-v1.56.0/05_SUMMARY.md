## Delivered on the re-vendor, with nothing asked for

- The slider and color-picker drag throttles work for this addon's nil-returning `scheduleTimer`
  (`C_Timer.After`): one commit per 50 ms window instead of one per frame (OptionsWidgets 31, review
  finding `KICKCD-R-19`).
- The Spells page's `ReorderList` drag polls on the library's ghost and takes its insertion line from
  a free list, so it no longer touches the pooled row frames' `OnUpdate` or paints a cached line
  into AceGUI's pool (Widgets 10).
- Banner and header pages stop leaking a widget and a texture per render (OptionsTabs 4).
- `CreateOptionsPanel` parks in combat and replays at `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` (Options 24).
  `OpenOptionsPanel` answers a boolean.
- The debug console's buffer trims in batches at the cap, not once per line (DebugLog 13).
- A leaked perf `Open` no longer parents a bracket in a later window (Perf 13).
- The launcher's missing-library notice prints once, without the `[LibKa0s] ` prefix (Launcher 2).
- `Core.Format` survives a secret value in a numeric slot (Core 8). `RegisterLSM` counts only what
  LSM holds (Media 4). The Bus tracking wrappers survive a newer AceEvent re-embed (Bus 2).
- Kit 26's stricter fakes: frames start shown, the AceDB fake raises on a bad profile name, a
  recording `EventRegistry`, `__badEvents`, the lone-CR gate, the store-root prose files, and case
  names carrying the section sign.
